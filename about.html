About us

We started Lumen
because most agency
work looks the same.

Founded in 2017, Lumen Studio is a small, senior team that believes good design comes from understanding a business deeply — not from applying a template.

Our story

From a spare room to a studio of nine

Lumen began as a two-person freelance partnership working out of a spare room, taking on small branding jobs for local shops. As word spread, the work grew into full website builds and long-term brand partnerships.

Today we're a team of nine designers, developers, and strategists working with clients across retail, hospitality, and professional services — but we've kept the same close, hands-on way of working we started with.

How we work

Small team, direct access

You work directly with the people designing your project — never through an account manager relaying messages. We keep every engagement small and focused so nothing gets lost in translation.
